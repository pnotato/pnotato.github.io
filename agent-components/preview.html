Graphic sandbox

15 self-contained SVG scenes plus eight HTML/CSS graphics. Every asset carries its own animation and needs no JavaScript.

The supplied planet and moon are preserved as separate HTML/CSS assets in html/. SVGs can be inlined with a raw import. To swap the live waves, use land-divider.svg and flip the reverse copy with scaleY(-1).

Checked background marks transparency. For a server, run python3 -m http.server in this folder.
