About Me

September 28, 2026

Hi, I’m Nick!

I’m currently a computer science and statistics student at Simon Fraser University. I have a big passion for technology and data architecture, with my interests spanning across distributed systems, cloud computing, and machine learning.

I’m an aspiring software engineer and researcher with various internship experiences under my belt, most notably at electric vehicle manufacturer Rivian & Volkswagen Group Technologies, Montreal-based ride-sharing startup Blaise Transit, and the Nobel laureate-founded BC Cancer Research Centre.

Outside of work, I’m a big fan of hiking, skiing, and enjoying the Vancouver outdoors.
